ABOUT US

A Tradition of Taste, Love, and Perseverance

Established in 2002, Cathy's Special Bibingka and Putobumbong began as a humble family business with a simple yet meaningful purpose: to provide additional income to support the educational needs of the owner's child, particularly tuition fees.

What started as a small source of extra income gradually grew into a livelihood through hard work, dedication, and perseverance. With every bibingka and putobumbong prepared, the business carries a story of family, sacrifice, and determination.

Rooted in Filipino culture and tradition, Cathy’s Special Bibingka and Putobumbong takes pride in serving beloved Filipino delicacies that bring warmth, comfort, and a taste of home.
